## Public-law source findings recorded 28 November 2026

A public-source scan of official Ethiopian Ministry of Justice publications identified General Education Proclamation No. 1368/2025, including Articles 21, 75 and 80 on religious-institution-owned general-education schools and school-level registration/licensing/recognition, and Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, including Articles 6, 7 and 11 on processing principles, lawful basis and minors' personal data. The school-specific current licensing status remains unknown; the Church registration remains the Owner-approved institutional-authority basis and is not treated as a substitute for school-level licensing. No further documents are requested from the Owner. See the official sources linked in the A1 baseline.

## Additional school licensing and fee rules recorded 28 November 2026

The public-source review of Directive No. 992/2016 recorded teacher licensing and school-leadership qualification requirements (Arts. 10–11), premises ownership/lease and infrastructure criteria (Art. 13), renewal lead time and reporting requirements (Art. 19), and registration-fee/tuition-increase controls (Art. 23). Amendment No. 1037/2025 adds a definition distinguishing tuition-fee increases from fee harmonization. Current Academy/staff/premises/fee compliance is not established. The system requirements track these rules without assuming current status or requesting more documents from the Owner.
